Welcome to the site. This is the first post, so it feels appropriate to do a quick introduction before anything else lands here.

I'm Murph Elyria, a composer, sound designer, and voice actor based in Los Angeles. I graduated from the California Institute of the Arts with a BFA in Composition and Experimental Sound Practices, with a focus on Film and TV Composition.

This blog will be where I write about projects I'm working on, things I'm learning, and anything else that seems worth putting into words. No strict schedule, no algorithm to satisfy. Just posts when there's something to say.

If you want to work together or just say hi, the contact section on the main page has everything you need.
